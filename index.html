Personality

Eren Yeager is a character defined by burning passion. From a young age, he craved freedom from the Walls that caged humanity and yearned for vengeance against the monstrous Titans that threatened their existence. This all-consuming desire fuels his unwavering determination. Eren possesses an almost superhuman drive, pushing himself relentlessly to achieve his goals. However, this fire can be a double-edged sword. Eren's emotions run hot, often leading him to make impulsive decisions and act recklessly. This tendency to charge headfirst into danger can be a valuable asset in combat situations, but it also puts himself and those around him at constant risk.

Despite his recklessness, Eren possesses a strong moral compass, especially when it comes to those he loves. He acts as a fierce protector for his adoptive siblings, Mikasa and Armin. Their safety is paramount to him, and any threat to them ignites a protective rage that fuels his actions. However, this protectiveness can morph into an obsessive focus. Once Eren sets his sights on a goal, particularly those tied to freedom or revenge, he can become fixated on achieving them. This single-mindedness can lead him down morally complex paths, blurring the lines between right and wrong as he grapples with the harsh realities of the world around him.

History

Timeline

Year 835
  • March 30th: Eren Yeager is born in Shiganshina District, Wall Maria, to Grisha and Carla Yeager.
Year 843
  • Eren witnesses a doctor treating a man seemingly possessed by a strange power, planting the seed of his fascination with Titans.
Year 845
  • March 1st: Grisha Yeager, possessing the Attack Titan power, steals the Founding Titan from the Reiss family.
  • March 2nd: Eren has a dream of himself seeing glimpses of future events.
  • Wall Maria is breached by the Colossal Titan and the Armored Titan. Eren witnesses his mother, Carla, being devoured by the Smiling Titan.
  • Eren and Mikasa find Armin after the attack and vow to join the military to fight Titans.
Year 847
  • Eren, Mikasa, and Armin join the 104th Training Corps.
Year 850
  • April: Eren undergoes intense training to master ODM gear and combat techniques.
  • Eren graduates fifth in his class, showcasing exceptional determination.
  • July 3rd: The Trost District Battle - Eren loses control and transforms into a Titan for the first time, breaching Wall Rose and unintentionally saving the Survey Corps.
  • Eren learns from his father's memories that he possesses the Attack Titan power and Grisha killed the Reiss family to obtain the Founding Titan.
Year 850 - 851
  • Eren undergoes training with Hange Zoe to control his Titan abilities.
  • Eren joins the Survey Corps' 57th expedition beyond Wall Maria. The expedition ends in disaster, with heavy losses.
Year 851
  • Eren participates in the Battle of Trost District against the Female Titan. Annie Leonhart, the Female Titan, captures Eren and attempts to escape with him.
  • The Survey Corps recaptures Eren after a fierce battle.
Year 853
  • Eren participates in the 5th Special Operations Squad mission to capture the Female Titan within Wall Sina.
  • Eren learns about Annie's backstory and motivations. Annie is encased in a crystal and placed in underground storage.
  • The Survey Corps discovers the truth about Marley, the nation responsible for sending Titans to Paradis Island.
Year 854
  • Eren travels with the Survey Corps to Marley undercover.
  • Eren reunites with his half-brother, Zeke Yeager, the inheritor of the Beast Titan.
  • Eren and Zeke form a temporary alliance to obtain the Founding Titan's power.
  • Eren returns to Paradis Island and reveals his plan to use the Founding Titan's power to activate the "Rumbling," unleashing the Wall Titans and wiping out humanity outside Paradis Island.
Year 854 - Present
  • Eren initiates the Rumbling, causing massive destruction and loss of life.
  • Eren's actions lead to a desperate struggle between his former comrades and the remaining forces of the world who oppose the Rumbling.
  • Eren and Zeke form a temporary alliance to obtain the Founding Titan's power.
  • During a final confrontation, Mikasa confronts Eren and, with a heavy heart, delivers a fatal blow. Eren dies, holding onto the dream of a world where he and his friends could be free.

Friends

Mikasa Ackerman

Orphaned by tragedy, Mikasa is a soldier of unwavering loyalty. Eren Yeager, her savior, becomes her charge. A stoic warrior with unmatched skill, Mikasa's quiet resolve shields her comrades. Her unwavering hope is a beacon against the Titans.

Armin Arlert

Driven by curiosity, Armin Arlert is a strategist with a thirst for knowledge. Despite his lack of physical strength, his keen intellect and resourcefulness make him invaluable. Armin's tactical mind and unwavering optimism become a beacon of hope for the Survey Corps, guiding them through the horrors unleashed by the Titans.

Jean Kirschtein

Jean Kirschtein is a talented soldier fueled by ambition. Initially arrogant and self-serving, his bravery and tactical prowess blossom under pressure. Jean's loyalty to his comrades strengthens over time, making him a reliable asset to the Survey Corps. Despite the darkness of the Titan threat, Jean's determination to protect his squad shines as a beacon of hope.

Sasha Blouse

Driven by a bottomless appetite, Sasha Blouse is a soldier with surprising ferocity. Despite her laid-back demeanor and obsession with food, Sasha possesses a keen marksmanship and unwavering loyalty. Her playful spirit and infectious enthusiasm lift the spirits of the Survey Corps, offering a spark of joy in the face of the ever-present threat of the Titans.

Connie Springer

A quick wit hides a well of emotion for Connie Springer. Driven by a desire to protect his family and a yearning for a better life, Connie's agility and resourcefulness make him a valuable member of the Survey Corps. Despite witnessing horrors, Connie's sense of humor and unwavering loyalty provide moments of levity and hope for his comrades in their fight against the Titans.

Historia Reiss

Cloaked in a mysterious past, Historia Reiss is a leader burdened by duty. Despite her gentle nature and initial self-doubt, Historia possesses a hidden strength and unwavering resolve. As queen, she fights for a better future for her people, her compassion and growing confidence becoming a beacon of hope in the face of the Titans and the harsh realities of the world.

Fun Facts

Clean Freak Titan

Eren's Titan form is surprisingly tidy. Unlike many other Titans who leave a trail of destruction, Eren's Titan is remarkably focused on its objective and avoids unnecessary collateral damage.

Short King

At the beginning of the series, Eren is actually one of the shortest characters. He stands at a mere 170 cm (5'5") but undergoes a growth spurt later on.

Dreaming of the Futures

Eren possesses a strange ability that allows him to see glimpses of the future through memories he inherits from the Attack Titan. This ability is a source of both fascination and frustration for him.

Surprisingly Popular

Despite his increasingly radical actions, Eren remains a surprisingly popular character among Attack on Titan fans. There are many theories about his motivations and his ultimate fate.

Hidden Meaning in his Name

The name Eren (エレン) can be translated from Japanese to mean "hopeful" or "blessed." This name choice takes on a complex meaning as the story progresses and Eren's actions become more morally ambiguous.
